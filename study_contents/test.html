UNSTABLE-ANGINA

Unstable Angina (UA) = chest pain at REST or with minimal exertion, unpredictable in pattern. Part of Acute Coronary Syndrome (ACS).

STEMRN • NCLEX REVIEW
Study Cards

SODIUM (Na⁺) - Hyponatremia <135

  • Nausea, confusion
  • Seizure risk ⚠️
  • SIADH → Fluid restriction
MANAGEMENT → Hypertonic saline (severe)

SODIUM (Na⁺) - Hypernatremia >145

  • Thirst, dehydration
  • Neuro changes
MANAGEMENT → Give fluids slowly (prevent cerebral edema)
